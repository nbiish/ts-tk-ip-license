# Records-Request Plan — Garden Island / Beaver Island Burial Grounds

**Purpose.** Obtain, digitally preserve, and organize the documentary record
of the Garden Island (Minis Gitigaan) burial-ground parcel: ownership history,
conveyances, site documentation, and the institutional actors involved.

**Discipline.**
- Facts and provenance only. No allegations in outbound requests; request
  records, not admissions.
- No FOIA where records are already public by law (deeds, corporate filings).
  FOIA = 5 U.S.C. § 552 (federal agencies) / MCL 15.231 et seq. (Michigan
  state bodies). County register of deeds: plain public-records copy request.
- Preserve every document received: 600 dpi PDF/A, SHA-256 checksum,
  source-and-date metadata note (see preservation protocol in
  `docs/affidavit-template.md`).

---

## Target 1 — The recorded deed (highest value, no FOIA needed)

- **Office:** Register of Deeds, Charlevoix County, Michigan (Garden Island
  is in Peaine Township, Charlevoix County). [Confirm current office address,
  phone, and copy fees on the county website before sending.]
- **Ask:** copy of all instruments for the burial-ground parcel (often cited
  as roughly 40 acres on Garden Island adjoining state-owned land); if the
  parcel number is unknown, request a grantee/grantor index search for
  "Miniss Kitigan Drum", "Peschel", and any prior owner names known to the
  family, covering the relevant decades.
- **Expected proof:** grantor, grantee, date, instrument type (warranty /
  quit-claim), consideration, legal description, liber/page or instrument
  number. This document anchors every later argument.
- **Cost:** small per-page fee; certified copy available for a bit more —
  get one certified copy for evidentiary use.

## Target 2 — NRHP nomination file (1978 listing, site 20CX12)

- **Sources:** Michigan State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) records
  request; National Park Service NPGallery digital archive (search "Garden
  Island Indian Cemetery"). [Look up the NRHP reference number during the
  search.]
- **Why:** nomination forms from that era state the property owner of record
  and describe the site's significance and integrity at listing — dated,
  official, and quotable.
- **Expected proof:** owner of record in 1978; site description; historical
  narrative (1851–1935 burial documentation cited in secondary sources).

## Target 3 — Original land patent / allotment history (free, online)

- **Source:** BLM General Land Office records (glorecords.blm.gov) — search
  Charlevoix County, Garden Island.
- **Why:** establishes whether the parcel originated as an 1855-Treaty-era
  Ottawa/Chippewa allotment or a cash-entry patent, and names the original
  patentee family.
- **Legal weight if allotment-derived:** conveyances out of restricted
  allotments without required approval are voidable; suits to cancel such
  conveyances historically run through the United States as trustee
  (Heckman v. United States, 224 U.S. 413 (1912)); limitation periods on
  U.S. trust-title suits are governed by 28 U.S.C. § 2415.
- **Expected proof:** patentee name, date, legal description, authority
  (allotment vs. sale).

## Target 4 — Miniss Kitigan Drum, Inc. institutional records (free, online)

- **Sources:** (a) Michigan LARA Corporations Online Filing System — entity
  search for incorporation date, registered agent, officers, status,
  annual reports; (b) if tax-exempt: IRS Form 990 series via ProPublica
  Nonprofit Explorer or Candid — officers, assets, property descriptions,
  mission statements.
- **Expected proof:** who controls the organization and when; any real
  property listed as organizational assets.

## Target 5 — Tribal and federal records (formal requests)

- **LTBB:** request (as a member or through GTB channels) copies of council
  resolutions/minutes referencing Garden Island, the cemetery parcel, or
  Miniss Kitigan Drum for the Ettawageshik tenure years. Support with the
  signed declaration (see `docs/affidavit-template.md`).
- **BIA (federal, FOIA 5 U.S.C. § 552):** agency files on allotments,
  probate/heirship records, or correspondence touching the parcel. Expect
  Privacy Act redactions; allottee probate records may require heir status.
- **Michigan DNR / state lands (Michigan FOIA)**: any state acquisition files
  adjoining the parcel; DNR management records for the Beaver Islands State
  Wildlife Research Area that reference the cemetery or third-party use.
- **GTB:** coordinate for parallel GTB records and for a joint historical
  record where the families agree.

## Working theory map (records → legal positions)

| Parcel was a restricted allotment sold without approval | Federal trust-title challenge (US as trustee); quiet-title analysis under 28 U.S.C. § 2415 |

| Federal nexus (funds, permits, federal land) at any point | NAGPRA (25 U.S.C. §§ 3001–3013; 43 CFR part 10) process hooks |
| Organizational asset listing of the parcel | Direct addressee for a return/co-stewardship demand letter |

## Reminder: strongest precedent path

A documented conveyance alone does not prove illegality. The
precedent-setting outcome with the best odds is a **negotiated return or
joint family–GTB–LTBB co-stewardship instrument**, memorialized in writing —
a result courts rarely produce but a documented record makes hard to refuse.
Litigation theories remain live in reserve and get stronger with every
record on file.
