# Witness Declaration Template — Garden Island Burial Grounds Records

**Purpose.** Convert firsthand testimony (e.g., from former LTBB Chairman Frank
Ettawageshik) into evidence-grade written form: dated, signed, specific, and
preserved with exhibits. A declaration like this is usable in tribal forums,
state/federal proceedings, repatriation and stewardship negotiations, and
historical documentation — regardless of which forum it ends up in.

**Rules.**
- Facts only. No characterizations of any person or organization.
- Every claim: who, what, when, where, how the witness knows.
- Attach or describe exhibits; do not rest on memory where a record exists.
- Notarization is optional but recommended (adds jurat credibility).

---

## DECLARATION OF [WITNESS FULL NAME]

I, [WITNESS FULL NAME], declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the
United States and the State of Michigan that the following is true and correct:

1.  **Identity and role.** I served as [title(s), e.g., Tribal Chairman] of the
    Little Traverse Bay Bands of Odawa Indians from [years/term dates].
    I currently reside in [city, state].

2.  **Basis of knowledge.** [Explain: personal participation, presence at
    council sessions, review of tribal records, conversations with named
    officials, etc. Specify for each block of facts how the witness knows.]

3.  **Subject property.** [Describe the parcel as precisely as known: name(s),
    e.g., Garden Island / Minis Gitigaan; approximate area; township and
    county (Peaine Township, Charlevoix County, Michigan); the burial ground
    and spirit houses located there; any legal description, parcel number, or
    plat reference if known.]

4.  **The conveyance.** [Facts: that the burial-ground parcel (or an interest
    in it) was conveyed by [grantor] to [grantee, e.g., Miniss Kitigan Drum,
    Inc. or associated person(s)]; approximate date; instrument type
    (deed/quit-claim/sale); consideration, if known; where and when the
    declarant learned of it; any county recording information known.]

5.  **Context.** [The declarant's statement of why the conveyance occurred,
    in the declarant's own words, attributed as testimony: e.g., "I told
    [name] that I [authorized/arranged/permitted] the transfer because
    ...". This section records the declarant's account; it does not assert
    legal conclusions.]

6.  **Associated records.** [Identify any tribal resolutions, minutes,
    correspondence, or files the declarant knows to exist relating to the
    conveyance, with approximate dates and where they are kept.]

7.  **Burial and cultural site condition.** [If within personal knowledge:
    described condition of the burial grounds and spirit houses at relevant
    dates; observed activities of third parties at the site; names/dates of
    observations; photographs (attached as Exhibits) with dates.]

8.  **Family and community statements.** [If relevant: statements by named
    Beaver Island Band family members about objections or protocols, with
    the declarant noting this was told to them and by whom/when.]

I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct.

## Preservation protocol (for whoever holds the signed original)

1. Scan at 600 dpi, PDF/A format.
2. Compute and record the SHA-256 checksum of the scan immediately:
   `shasum -a 256 decl.pdf > decl.pdf.sha256`
3. Retain the original in safe physical custody; record the chain of custody
   (who holds it, transfers, dates).
4. Distribute only copies; the original stays in custody.
